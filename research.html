Research

Efficient models for high-impact vision problems.

Efficient AI Architectures

Designing low-complexity deep learning models that preserve accuracy while reducing computation through transform-based layers, decorrelated attention, and state-space modeling.

Medical Imaging

Developing MRI reconstruction, segmentation, and prostate cancer analysis pipelines with hybrid residual, Hadamard, and Mamba-based architectures.

Remote Sensing

Applying transfer learning and computer vision to wildfire monitoring, situational awareness, and robust environmental detection under challenging data conditions.

Hardware-Aware ML

Exploring GPU training, FPGA deployment, high-level synthesis, and model designs suited for constrained computing systems.
